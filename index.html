FARE COMPARE

Same ride. Half the fare.

The same ride in a Pakistani city costs wildly different fares on different apps — and nobody compares before booking. Mahnoor, 22, from Lahore — a university student who rides daily — paid Rs 580 last week for a ride that cost Rs 320 the day before.

SURGE PLANNER

Fog evening? Wait 25 min.

In evening fog the surge hits ×1.8 and Careem jumps from Rs 460 to Rs 828. Fog season is Nov–Feb — fewer drivers, higher surge. Check the forecast by hour, then wait for it to drop.

SAFE RIDE

Check 5 points. Ride safe.

A driver cancelled twice on Mahnoor after accepting at 10pm. Tick every check off before you book; your ticks are saved on this device. She shares her live trip with her brother every night.

    Driver cancellation rate

    A driver who cancelled 2 of the last 10 rides is flagged a frequent canceller. Check the rate before you book.

    Share live trip

    Your family follows the ride live — the same way Mahnoor shares hers with her brother every night. Saved on this device.

    BOOK A RIDE

    Booked. Rs 320, inDrive.

    Book the cheapest verified ride and get a booking reference with a live ETA — no more paying Rs 580 for a Rs 320 ride. Pay in the app: no cash, no "petrol money".

    FARE SPOTTER

    Spot the red flags.

    Three things a driver does that should stop you getting in — and one safe habit. Call each one a red flag or safe, then check your score.
